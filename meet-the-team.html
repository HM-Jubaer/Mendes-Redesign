    Meet the Team

    Our experienced and talented dental team

    Our experienced and talented dental team is committed to working with you and your family to create a comfortable, stress-free, and rewarding dental experience every time you visit our practice.

    We are pleased to welcome you and your family, and we look forward to working with you.

    Dr. Charles H. Mendes, DDSDoctorate of Dental Surgery – Creighton University School of Dentistry, 1973Meet Dr. Charles Dr. Paul M. Mendes, DDSDoctorate of Dental Surgery – Creighton University School of Dentistry, 2006Meet Dr. Paul
    01

    Paulina

    Dental Assistant

    I work with Dr. Paul as a chairside assistant: taking X-rays, polishing teeth, setting up for procedures. I am also responsible for the sterilization and organization of our dental instruments. Patient interaction is the best part of my job! Each person is a unique individual with so much to share. I also love working as a team with Dr. Paul and my coworkers. We get along great!

    I’m originally from Mexico and love Minnesota because I have more possibilities to meet people from all over the world. I love it when I get the opportunity to translate for our spanish speaking patients.

    02

    Sharon

    Financial Coordinator

    I am here to assist patients in understanding their insurance benefits and making financial arrangements that fit their needs. I get great satisfaction in helping our patients receive the quality care they deserve. I was trained as a dental assistant and worked in that capacity for more than 30 years before transitioning to the front desk. My years of clinical experience really come in handy when dealing with insurance companies!

    I have a son and daughter who are grown and living on their own. I have a dog named Chloe to keep me company at home. I enjoy spending time with my family and friends, and lounging at the pool.

    03

    Stephanie

    Dental Hygienist

    Making sure my patients achieve ideal dental health is my number-one priority. We go over nutrition, good home care, and the importance of regular cleanings and checkups. I like getting to meet new people every day and helping each person maintain a healthy smile.

    I really like being outside and horseback riding: I have two horses named Teddy and Bizzy. I also have two dogs, Murphy and Clover. I enjoy going to the lake, spending time with friends and family, and doing crafts.

    04

    Barb

    Front Desk Receptionist / Assistant

    It is my goal to keep our office running smoothly by keeping patients scheduled for necessary dental work, keeping the doctors on time, and managing recall appointments. My career in dentistry goes back 40 years, and I’ve loved the variety that each day has brought. There is nothing better than seeing patients leave knowing they are happy with their dental work.

    I am a new grandmother to my son-in-law and daughter’s son, Milo. Being a grandparent is truly a blessing. I like golfing and being with family and friends.

    05

    Nancy

    Dental Assistant

    It has been my pleasure to be a part of this office and serve our wonderful patients for more than 40 years. I help keep the office organized by ordering supplies and maintaining our equipment and instruments, but most importantly, I help to make patients as comfortable as possible during their dental procedures. I’ve enjoyed getting to know our family of patients on a personal level over the years and helping them maintain healthy mouths.

    I’m married with two grown children and adorable twin grandsons. My hobbies include traveling, cooking, biking, and scrap booking.
