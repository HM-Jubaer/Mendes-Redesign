    Welcome To

    Mendes Family Dentistry

    For more than 40 years, Dr. Charles H. Mendes, Dr. Paul M. Mendes, and our team have been dedicated to meeting our patients’ unique dental needs with pain-free, modern dentistry.

    Our practice has a reputation for being family-oriented, friendly, and comfortable, and we strive to maintain this reputation by offering a variety of high-quality general and cosmetic treatments.

    • Preventive cleanings and exams
    • Cosmetic dentistry with teeth whitening and veneers
    • Crowns, bridges, dentures, and implants
    • Gum disease treatment
    • Fillings, root canals, and extractions

    What Sets Us Apart

    Quality, patient-focused dental care

    We believe in treating our patients as we would our own families, with excellent patient care, respect for our patients’ needs and time, and a genuine concern for our patients’ health and well-being.

    01

    Your comfort

    We are very committed to relationship building and allaying our patients’ fears and concerns in the dental office. We provide a spa-like experience with audio relaxation therapy and nitrous oxide analgesia to help make our patients feel more comfortable.

    02

    Your convenience

    We are a full-service practice with 24/7 emergency coverage. We take care of all needs for more than 100 insurance companies.

    03

    Your experience

    We want our patients to feel confident in their health and be so happy and pleased with our services that they want to refer their family, friends, and coworkers.
