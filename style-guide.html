Brand analysis

The logo is the anchor. It is classic and trustworthy: a single deep navy, a Roman-style serif wordmark, and a tooth set inside a circle. The redesign keeps all of that and adds what the old site lacks: warmth, light, air, and modern UI.

1
The mark: tooth in a circle

A white tooth silhouette with an "MFD" monogram on a navy disc. It works as an icon, favicon, and avatar, and it inspired our circular icon buttons.

2
Classical serif wordmark

"MENDES" is set in flared, Trajan-style capitals. We echo it with Marcellus for display type, so headlines feel like they belong to the logo.

3
One colour: Mendes Navy #233D85

Sampled straight from the artwork. It reads as established, calm, and clinical. Everything in the palette is built around it.

4
The vertical rule

A thin divider separates mark and name. We reuse it as a quiet detail: hairline dividers and the eyebrow rule.

Brand personality

Hometown

A neighbourhood practice on Snelling Ave since the 1970s, not a chain.

Family, literally

Father and son dentists, with four generations of patients.

Trusted & established

Navy, serif, and calm layouts. Confident, never loud.

Warm & modern

Light sky blues, generous radius, and real smiles. "We love making people smile!"

08

Imagery

Real people, real smiles, bright rooms. Photography is warm and naturally lit, with whites and soft blues that sit easily on the Sky palette. Use the practice's own photos of Dr. Charles, Dr. Paul, the team, and the Snelling Ave office wherever possible. The stock images in these mockups are placeholders.

Genuine smiles
Arch mask
Bright spaces
  • Shapes: 24px rounded rectangles, arch tops (the reference "Nuvica" hero), and an organic blob for hero portraits
  • Floating glass chips on photos carry proof points such as "Since 1973" and "24/7 emergency"
  • Navy overlay at 55% when text sits on a photo
  • Shoot list: both doctors together, each doctor chairside, the team at the front desk, the building on Snelling Ave, the treatment rooms
  • Avoid: cold blue clinical stock, gloved hands with drills, gaping mouths, and fake-looking 3D teeth
  • Export WebP at 1600px max and under 250KB for Core Web Vitals
09

Voice & tone

Talk like Dr. Charles and Dr. Paul talk to patients: warm, plain-spoken, reassuring, and a little proud of their hometown. Lead with people and comfort, not equipment.

We say

"Teeth for a Lifetime. Remember, you don't get a third set."

"Your family's hometown dental team on Snelling Ave."

"Nervous? Ask about nitrous oxide and audio relaxation."

We avoid

"State-of-the-art, world-class, cutting-edge solutions."

"Best dentist in Minnesota, guaranteed results."

Jargon without explanation, such as "endodontic therapy" without "root canal".

Key messages (from the practice's own content)

Father & son

Dr. Charles (DDS 1973) and Dr. Paul (DDS 2006), with four generations of patients.

Comfort first

A spa-like experience with audio relaxation therapy and nitrous oxide. Pain-free, modern dentistry.

Here when you need us

24/7 emergency coverage. More than 100 insurance companies. Spanish-speaking help.

Reference analysis

What we took from each preferred design, and how it is translated into the Mendes brand.

Ref 1: "Denta Care" landing page

Oversized display headline behind the hero, a soft sky hero panel, stats on the right, and a pill CTA with an arrow.

  • Kinetic marquee strip of treatments → used in V2
  • Pastel treatment cards with photo and tags → V2 treatments rail
  • Grey-to-colour team carousel and pill-input booking form → V2
  • Giant wordmark in the footer → "MENDES" footer in V2

Ref 2: "Meet the minds behind your smile"

A blue framed specialist slider with role tags, short bios, "practicing since", and a 01/17 counter.

  • Doctor card component: navy card, photo, tag, and a "since" line → all versions
  • Slider with a counter and arrow buttons → V3 team section
  • Avatar stack (+14) → glass chips in the hero

Ref 3: "Nuvica" medical

Airy blue-white gradients, glassmorphism stat chips, an arch-masked portrait, a three-card service row with arrow circles, and a big stats row.

  • Glass chips floating on the hero image → V3 (and V1 accents)
  • Arch photo mask → V3 hero, imagery rules
  • "From 28/10/2003" card on the building image → "Since 1973" card in V3

Ref 4: "PureSmile" implant center

A clean white layout, a blob-masked hero photo, a bento grid of mixed cards, and a soft form block beside a happy-patient photo.

  • Bento grid of services and proof points → V1
  • Blob hero and a two-tone headline (navy + blue) → V1
  • "Ready to transform your smile?" form card → V1 booking block
